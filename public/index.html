How it works

  1. 1

    Build

    Choose your plan, translation, start date, and how many chapters to read each day.

  2. 2

    Subscribe

    Add the URL to your RSS reader. There's no account or email to sign up with.

  3. 3

    Read

    New chapters appear each day. Tap one to open it on Bible Gateway.

Build the URL by hand

The feed URL follows this pattern:

/rssbible/{plan}/{translation}/{yyyyMMdd}/{chapters}/feed.rss
  • plan: ot, nt, or full
  • translation: any code from the translation list, such as esv or kjv
  • yyyyMMdd: the start date, so June 5th, 2026 is 20260605. Use yesterday's date to start on day 1 today.
  • chapters: chapters per day, from 1 to 99

Example: the whole Bible in the ESV, 5 chapters a day, starting January 1st, 2026:

https://www.bibleplanfeed.com/rssbible/full/esv/20260101/5/feed.rss
